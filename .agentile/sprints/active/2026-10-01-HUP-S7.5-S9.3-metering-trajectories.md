## Decisions taken conservatively (for owner review)

1. `capsule_id` for Hermes metrics is `keccak256("citrate.hermes.agent-loop.v1")`; metric names
   are `keccak256("hermes.daily.<metric>")`; rates are basis points; undefined metrics are omitted.
2. Opt-in is a constructed `BenchmarkOptIn` (no default, no flag); the registry address is a
   caller input because BenchmarkRegistry is not in the 40204 address book yet.
3. Taint excludes the **whole session** from export, including turns before the taint and after
   a member clears it. Allowing it needs a non-empty reason, written into the report.
4. All 32-byte hex is redacted as a secret (tx hashes and digests included).
5. Paths inside granted roots are kept as `[root:N]/...`, never with their absolute prefix.
6. Model-invented tool names are pooled as `(unknown tool)` in metering records.

## Journal

The loop's event stream turned out to carry almost everything metering needs, with one wrinkle:
the workflow runner emits `verifier` events *after* the attempt's `done`. Treating `done` as the
end of a record would have silently dropped every verdict; the sinks attach verdicts to the turn
that just closed, and a test drives a real two-attempt workflow to hold that in place.

The other lesson was about what counts as "content". A tool name looks like metadata, but a
small model can call a tool named after the user's sentence. Pooling unknown names, dropping
verifier failure details (they quote answers) and planting canaries in every field gave the
"no content" claim a test instead of a promise.

Redaction found its first real bug through its own test: a seed phrase ending a sentence, followed
by "keep" (also a BIP-39 word), let the run swallow the start of the next sentence. Periods now join words only
inside numbered lists.
